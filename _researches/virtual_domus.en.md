Virtual-Domus is a research project focused on studying the effects of mixed reality environments on manual skills, functionality, and quality of life in people with multiple sclerosis.

Our role focuses on the design and development of interactive mixed reality experiences used as part of a controlled clinical study in the field of physiotherapy.
