**Alberto H** is a Spanish game producer and artist credited on classic-era titles with **Dinamic Multimedia**, including work as Executive Producer on games like **Runaway: A Road Adventure** and **ExcaliBug**. He later earned credits with major publishers such as **Electronic Arts**.
