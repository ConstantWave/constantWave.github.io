**Gryzor87** is a Spanish composer specialized in **retro/arcade** style music and “chip/old-school” audio. Among his best-known works are the soundtracks for **Cursed Castilla (Maldita Castilla EX)**, **Super Hydorah**, and **Battle Princess Madelyn**, among others.
